Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Painting Business OS respects your privacy. This application operates with a client-first approach to data protection:

  • Local Data Storage: All client details, job estimations, quotes, invoices, and business figures created in Painting Business OS are stored locally on your device unless synced via your personal iCloud storage.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not sell, rent, or share your business data, client details, or quotes with third parties or advertising networks.
  • Payments & Subscriptions: All payments and auto-renewable subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's secure In-App Purchase (StoreKit) system. We do not collect or store your credit card details.
